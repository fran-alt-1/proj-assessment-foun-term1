What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services such as storage, servers, software, and databases over the internet. It allows users and organisations to access technology resources without relying only on their own local computers or hardware.

Cloud Storage

Data and files can be stored online and accessed from different devices through the internet.

Scalability

Cloud resources can be increased or reduced depending on the needs of an organisation.

Remote Access

Users can access cloud based applications, files, and services from different locations with an internet connection.
