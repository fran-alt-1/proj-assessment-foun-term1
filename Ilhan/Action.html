Green Computing In Action

Green technology, also known as sustainable IT, refers to the development and use of technologies that aims to minimize the negative impacts of human activities on the environment and society throughout their life cycle. It encompasses a wide range of products, services and practices that support a more sustainable future.

Business

Businesses use green computing to reduce the environmental impact of their digital operations while lowering energy costs and hardware waste.


How Businesses Utilise Green Computing

Server Virtualization: Companies run multiple virtual machines on a single physical server, consolidating workloads to reduce the total number of physical machines powered on.


Government

Governments use green computing by implementing energy-efficient IT infrastructure, sustainable procurement policies, and digital waste reduction programs to lower public sector carbon emissions.


How The Government Utilises Green Computing

Data Center Optimization: Agencies consolidate physical servers using virtualization and migrate legacy systems to shared cloud solutions that utilize advanced cooling technologies, such as hot or cold aisle containment, and renewable energy sources.


Education

Green IT in education refers to the adoption of environmentally sustainable practices in the use of information technology within educational institutions. It encompasses strategies to minimize energy consumption, reduce electronic waste, and promote the use of eco-friendly technologies.


How Green Computing is utilised in Education

Energy Efficiency: Many schools prioritise purchasing devices with high efficiency ratings, such as ENERGY STAR or EPEAT (Electronic Product Environmental Assessment Tool) certified computers, laptops, and tablets. These devices use significantly less power both when active and when idle and is more than capable for schoolwork.
