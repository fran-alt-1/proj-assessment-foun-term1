Welcome to the Digital Workplace Readiness Portal

An online learning portal that helps students, graduates, and employees build the workplace technology knowledge, digital skills, and awareness of emerging IT trends they need to succeed, with a special focus on Green Computing.

About the Portal

Technology is changing how we work. The Digital Workplace Readiness Portal gives you clear, practical lessons on the tools and skills used in modern workplaces, and shows how energy-efficient and sustainable computing is shaping the future of IT.

Who the Portal Is For

  • Students: Build digital skills and good technology habits before you start work.
  • Graduates: Connect your studies to the skills employers are looking for.
  • Employees: Keep your skills current as workplace tools and technologies change.

What Is Green Computing?

Green Computing is the practice of designing and using computers, software, and data centers in ways that reduce energy use and environmental impact. It is one of the most important emerging trends in IT, and many company recommend their workers to have basic understanding of it.

  • Reduce energy use with efficient devices and settings.
  • Understand how cloud services and data centers affect the environment.
  • Extend the life of equipment and recycle electronic waste responsibly.
  • Prepare for careers in sustainable technology.
