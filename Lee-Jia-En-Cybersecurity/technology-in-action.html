PAGE 03 · TECHNOLOGY IN ACTION

Cybersecurity protects real people and services

Security is useful anywhere people depend on information and connected technology.

EDU

Education

Schools and universities store student records, assignment files and account details. Unique accounts, access permissions, software updates and backups help protect that information. Students can reduce phishing risk by checking unexpected messages before signing in.

Example: a lecturer shares a course folder only with the correct class and enables MFA on the staff account.

BUS

Business

Businesses use email, cloud documents, payment services and customer records. Cybersecurity can protect these systems through role-based access, secure configuration, staff awareness, monitoring and recovery planning. NIST CSF 2.0 gives organisations a flexible way to organise risk-management outcomes (NIST, 2024).

Example: a finance team verifies unusual payment requests through a second trusted channel.

HLT

Healthcare

Clinics and hospitals need to protect health records and keep essential services available. Access controls help limit who can see sensitive records; backups and response plans help teams continue care during technology disruptions.

Example: staff use individual accounts and approved devices, and quickly report a suspicious login alert.

LIFE

Daily life

People use phones for banking, messaging, shopping and personal photographs. Strong unique passwords, MFA, updates and careful link checking help reduce common account risks (CISA, n.d.).

Example: a person checks a bank's official app instead of following a surprise text message link.

A SMALL INCIDENT, A CLEAR RESPONSE

What should happen after a suspicious click?

  1. Stop. Do not enter more information or forward the message.
  2. Report. Tell the organisation's IT or security contact using its official process.
  3. Protect. Follow their instructions; change a password from a trusted device if asked.
  4. Recover and learn. Check affected accounts, restore needed work and share what helped prevent further harm.
